Reported UPI IDs

Paste UPI IDs you know are fraudulent (for example ones you have reported at cybercrime.gov.in), one per line. Paying any of them sets the mule signal to maximum.

On a phone, “on a call” and “screen sharing” would come from the operating system. A web page can’t read them, so tick them yourself when testing.

Score your real statement

Export a CSV statement from your bank’s net banking or UPI app and add it here. It is read in this browser only and never uploaded. Outgoing payments are scored one by one in date order, with your profile learned from earlier payments.

What a statement can and can’t show

Columns are detected automatically: date, optional time, description or narration, and debit/withdrawal or amount with a Dr/Cr type. UPI IDs are read from the description.

A statement has no device or call information, so those signals stay off. Scoring uses amount spikes, new payees, unusual hours (when the statement has times), bursts of payments and your reported-ID list.
